# Product Brief

## Problem

AI builders face too much research noise. Papers, model releases, benchmarks, repos, and commentary arrive faster than a small team can evaluate. Most tracking workflows are either passive bookmarks or generic newsletters.

## Solution

AI Research Radar provides a focused dashboard for ranking AI developments by implementation value, novelty, risk, and strategic relevance. It treats research as a product and engineering input, not as a reading list.

## Target Users

- Solo AI builders.
- Startup founders evaluating model capabilities.
- Product engineers looking for practical experiments.
- Research-minded operators who need concise weekly briefs.

## Differentiation

The project does not simply summarize articles. It converts signals into decisions:

- Which idea should be tested?
- What engineering work would prove it?
- What risk could block adoption?
- How does it fit the current AI stack?

## Near-Term Roadmap

2. Add saved scoring rubrics.
3. Add AI-generated weekly brief export.
4. Add experiment outcomes and model comparison notes.
